GASFACTS_ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

GASFACTS_ is a field utility application designed for gasfitters and plumbers. This privacy policy explains how GASFACTS_ handles information when you use the application.

Information stored by GASFACTS_

GASFACTS_ may store information that you enter into the application, including calculation inputs, assessment information, notes, images, and saved job or report data.

This information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share it using features provided by your device.

Personal information

GASFACTS_ does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through an external GASFACTS_ server.

Images and files

If you choose to add images, notes, or other information to GASFACTS_, that information is used to provide the application's reporting and record-keeping features.

GASFACTS_ does not automatically upload these files to the developer. You control any export or sharing of information from the application.

Data sharing

GASFACTS_ does not sell user data or personal information.

Information is not shared with the developer or third parties by GASFACTS_ unless you deliberately use your device's sharing or export functionality to send that information elsewhere.

Internet access

Core GASFACTS_ calculation and field functionality is designed to operate locally on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Information saved within GASFACTS_ remains on your device until you delete it through the application, remove the application's data, or uninstall the application, subject to your device's own backup and storage behaviour.

Children

GASFACTS_ is a professional trade utility and is not designed for or directed toward children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if GASFACTS_ features or data-handling practices change. The current version will be available at this webpage.

Contact

For privacy questions relating to GASFACTS_, contact: gasfactsapp@gmail.com
